OUR STORY

Serving Education & Spiritual Literature

Nkhungudzu Bookshop was established on Thursday, 4 October 2012.

The bookshop was established to serve people through education and spiritual literature. Today Nkhungudzu Bookshop provides books, office stationery and spiritual literature to customers across Malawi.

The bookshop serves Christian schools and institutions and has contributed to educating vulnerable children through different levels of education, including universities.

Nkhungudzu Bookshop's main products include Bibles of different versions, educational books and stationery.
